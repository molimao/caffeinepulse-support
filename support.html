快速记录

第一次选择饮品后，按显示的参考份量和当前时间记入。之后首页会显示最近的一杯，点一次即可再记。成功后可以撤销这一杯，或明确再记一杯。

份量与补记

需要时再改半份、一份或两份，或把时间改到实际喝下的时刻。取消不会产生记录。

历史中的编辑与删除

记录按日期列出。可以改一条已有记录，或删除它。删除会去掉这条本机记录。

存储

记录和偏好只在本机，并排除系统备份。卸载 App 会删除这些本机数据。没有账号，也不会把记录上传到服务器。

估算

227 款预设的咖啡因数值是估算，没有逐一核验全部品牌。曲线是模型估算的体内咖啡因量，不是血液浓度，不能保证入睡，也不是实时健康测量。半衰期默认 5 小时，可在 1 到 24 小时之间调整，只用于比较。

Quick record

The first time, choose a drink and save it at the shown reference portion and the current time. After that, the home screen shows the latest drink and one tap records it again. After a successful save you can undo that cup or explicitly log another.

Portion and time

Change to a half, full, or double portion, or set the time you actually drank it, only when you need to. Cancelling does not create a record.

Edit and delete in History

History lists records by day. You can edit an existing entry or delete it. Deleting removes that on-device record.

Storage

Records and preferences stay on the device and are excluded from system backup. Uninstalling the app removes that on-device data. There is no account, and records are not uploaded.

Estimates

The 227 presets use estimated caffeine amounts. They have not been rechecked for every brand. The curve is a model estimate of caffeine in the body. It is not a blood level, a sleep guarantee, or a real-time health measurement. The default half-life is 5 hours and can be set from 1 to 24 hours for comparison only.
