原生 App「下一杯」

  • 饮品记录和偏好只保存在这台设备上，并排除系统备份。
  • 不收集、不上传个人数据。没有账号、广告或跟踪。
  • 不申请通知、健康、定位或其他系统权限。不需要健康记录或病历。
  • App 内的隐私政策页面不访问外部网络。
  • 在记录中删除一条，会删除该条本机记录。卸载 App 会删除本机保存的数据。
  • 咖啡因数值是估算，不是医学结论，也不保证睡眠，更不是实时健康测量。
  • 界面为简体中文。

已有网站

已有网站是另一项服务，可以有自己的账号和自己保存的数据。那些账号、登录和网站数据不适用于「下一杯」的本机记录。请阅读该网站自己的政策。本页不是网站政策。

支持邮件与本页托管

如果你自愿发支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件正文，只用于回复并处理该次请求。不会为此索取咖啡因摄入记录或健康记录。你可以再发邮件，要求删除这些支持往来。App 本身仍不会自动发送记录。这些帮助页面不能读取保存在 App 内的记录。App 不会收集或上传这些记录。

本页与支持说明是静态网页。托管服务（例如 GitHub Pages）可能按其自己的政策处理普通网页访问日志。App 内的政策页面离线显示，不联系托管服务。

English

Last updated: 2026-10-23. This page covers only the native iPhone app Next Cup. It does not cover the existing website. The native app does not sign in to that website and does not use website accounts. For the website, read that site’s own policy. Support

Native app

  • Drink records and preferences stay on the device and are excluded from system backup.
  • The app does not collect or upload personal data. It has no account, ads, or tracking.
  • It does not request notification, Health, location, or other system permissions. Health records are not required.
  • The in-app privacy policy does not contact an external network.
  • Deleting an entry in History removes that on-device record. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device data.
  • Caffeine figures are estimates. They are not a medical conclusion, a sleep guarantee, or a real-time health measurement.
  • The interface is Simplified Chinese.

Existing website

The existing website is a separate service. It may have its own accounts and its own stored data. Those accounts, sign-in, and website data do not apply to Next Cup’s on-device log. Use the website’s own notice. This page is not that notice.

Support email and this page

If you choose to send a support email, the developer receives your email address and the message, and uses them only to reply and handle that request. The developer does not ask for coffee intake or health records for that purpose. You may email again to request deletion of that support correspondence. The app itself still does not send records automatically. These help pages cannot read the records stored in the app. The app does not collect or upload those records.

This page and the support page are static. A hosting provider (for example GitHub Pages) may process ordinary web access logs under its own policy. The in-app policy screen is offline and does not contact the hosting provider.
